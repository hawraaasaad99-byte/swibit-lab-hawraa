# AI Ethics and Responsible Development Policy

## 1. Principles of Responsible AI
Our engineering and data science divisions are bound by strict ethical guidelines to ensure that all artificial intelligence models developed, deployed, or maintained by our company promote fairness, accountability, transparency, and safety. We reject the development of models intended for malicious surveillance, autonomous warfare, or intentional misinformation generation.

## 2. Fairness, Bias Mitigation, and Evaluation
- **Dataset Auditing:** AI engineering teams must actively evaluate and audit training datasets for historical, statistical, and demographic biases before initiating any model training jobs.
- **Fairness Metrics:** Quantitative fairness metrics (such as disparate impact and equalized odds) must be documented in model cards and reviewed quarterly by the Ethics Committee.
- **Remediation:** If significant bias is detected post-deployment, the affected model must be rolled back or patched within 48 hours.

## 3. Transparency, Explainability, and Human Oversight
- **Explainability Features:** High-stakes AI outputs—such as automated financial scoring, medical diagnosis assistance, or legal risk assessment—must incorporate explainability features, attention weights, or confidence intervals.
- **Right to Human Review:** Clients and end-users maintain the absolute right to request an immediate human review of any automated decision or classification generated by our systems, bypassing automated bottlenecks entirely.
