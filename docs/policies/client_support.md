# Client Support and Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

## 1. Support Tiers, Coverage, and Response Times
Our client success and technical engineering teams operate across structured support tiers to resolve integration bugs, infrastructure failures, and model degradation incidents efficiently:
- **Tier 1 (Critical Production Outages):** Applies to complete system downtime, API gateway failures, or security breaches. Guaranteed response time is within 15 minutes, with 24/7 round-the-clock engineering coverage.
- **Tier 2 (Model Performance Degradation):** Applies to severe latency spikes, high error rates, or unexpected model hallucination loops affecting production. Guaranteed response time is within 2 hours during business days.
- **Tier 3 (General Inquiries and Feature Requests):** Applies to general API usage questions, documentation clarifications, and billing inquiries. Guaranteed response time is within 24 business hours.

## 2. Incident Escalation Workflow
If an AI model failure or hallucination causes a critical client workflow disruption or data corruption, support engineers must immediately escalate the ticket to the Lead AI Engineer on-call via PagerDuty. A post-mortem report must be published within 48 hours of resolution.
