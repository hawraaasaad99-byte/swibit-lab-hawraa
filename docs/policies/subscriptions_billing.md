# Subscriptions, Billing, and Refund Policy

## 1. Subscription Tiers and Usage Limits
Our AI Solutions platform provides scalable subscription tiers billed on a monthly or annual commitment basis:
- **Developer Tier:** A complimentary entry-level tier limited strictly to 10,000 API calls per month, featuring standard community forum support and shared vector search infrastructure.
- **Pro Tier:** Priced at $99 per month, tailored for growing startups and medium-scale applications. Includes up to 500,000 API calls monthly, priority vector search routing, automated backups, and standard email support with a 24-hour response window.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Custom negotiated pricing designed for large-scale operations. Includes dedicated GPU compute clusters, custom fine-tuned LLM models, bespoke SLA guarantees, and dedicated 24/7 phone and Slack support channels.

## 2. Automatic Renewal, Upgrades, and Billing Cycles
- **Auto-Renewal:** All paid subscription tiers renew automatically at the conclusion of each billing cycle unless formal cancellation notice is submitted at least 24 hours prior to the renewal timestamp.
- **Proration on Upgrades:** Customers may upgrade or downgrade their tier seamlessly through the user dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately, applying prorated credit charges for the remainder of the active billing period.

## 3. Cancellation Terms and Refund Eligibility
- Users may terminate their subscription at any time. Cancellation prevents future recurring charges, but no partial refunds or credits are issued for unused days within the current active billing month.
- **Refund Exceptions:** Financial refunds are evaluated strictly on a case-by-case basis and are granted solely in the event of proven, extended platform outages that violate our guaranteed Enterprise SLA uptime commitments.
